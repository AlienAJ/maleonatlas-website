About Maleon Atlas

Maleon Atlas is a Canada-to-Kenya parcel forwarding pilot service. We help customers in Kenya shop from Canadian retailers by receiving their packages in Canada and forwarding them to Kenya. For now, all quotes are handled manually through WhatsApp so we can confirm the item, weight, shipping options and customs considerations before you pay.

We are not a full shipping company or customs broker. This is a concierge-style pilot service. Canada-side logistics and coordination are provided by Longonot Group Inc.

How It Works

  1. Contact Us: Send us a WhatsApp message with your name, city, phone number, item link or description, estimated price and weight, and whether you are purchasing yourself or need us to buy on your behalf.
  2. Order & Ship: Place your order with the Canadian retailer and use our Canadian receiving address. We provide this only after your initial item review.
  3. We Receive & Inspect: We receive your package in Canada, verify that it matches your description, take a photo, weigh and measure it, and prepare a shipping quote.
  4. Get a Quote: We present available shipping options, our handling fee, packaging or insurance costs, and any additional services. You approve and pay before we ship.
  5. Delivery in Kenya: Your parcel is shipped to Kenya. Depending on the courier, you may need to pay duties and taxes upon arrival.

Fees & Services

Every quote separates the costs clearly. Typical charges include:

  • Handling Fee: CAD $15-$25 per package, covering receiving, storage and basic prep.
  • Personal Shopping: 10%-15% of item value, minimum CAD $20, if we purchase on your behalf.
  • Consolidation & Repacking: CAD $5-$10 per additional package and CAD $5-$15 for repacking if needed.
  • Shipping Cost: Based on weight, dimensions, service level and carrier, charged at cost.
  • Insurance: Optional for high-value items.
Customs & Duties: Duties, VAT and customs fees in Kenya are not included unless we specifically state otherwise. These may be collected by the courier or customs before delivery.

Items We Accept

We forward most non-perishable consumer goods. We do not handle:

  • Alcohol, tobacco, cannabis or drugs
  • Weapons or hazardous goods
  • Prescription medication
  • Perishable food
  • Counterfeit or restricted goods
  • High-value electronics without prior agreement
