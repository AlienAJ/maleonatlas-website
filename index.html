Behind-the-Counter EV Parts Supply

Maleon Atlas Parts helps Kenyan spare parts dealers and garages add EV-specific parts to their catalogue without building overseas supplier relationships from scratch.

We focus on fitment-sensitive EV components and accessories that traditional ICE parts markets may not reliably stock yet, including charging cables, EV-safe fluids, cabin filters, Nissan Leaf components, coolant pumps, DC-DC converters, and selected high-voltage service parts.

Our role is simple: source, verify, quote, and supply. You keep your customer relationship and add your own margin.

How It Works

  1. Send the Request: Share the vehicle model, year, photos, part number if available, and customer urgency.
  2. We Check Fitment: We review the part request and confirm whether it should be stocked, sourced locally, or sourced from overseas.
  3. Receive Trade Quote: We provide a trade price, estimated availability, and sourcing route where possible.
  4. You Keep the Customer: You quote and sell to your customer under your own relationship and margin.
  5. We Supply or Source: Maleon Atlas coordinates the part supply through trusted channels in China, the UK, Japan, Canada, UAE, or Kenya depending on fitment and urgency.

Why This Exists

Kenya’s traditional auto parts market is strong in ICE vehicles. EVs create a different challenge: charging standards, high-voltage components, thermal systems, software-sensitive modules, and new Chinese EV fitments.

Maleon Atlas Parts is built to sit upstream of the trade as a focused EV parts sourcing desk. We are not trying to replace established parts dealers. We help them serve EV customers before the market becomes fully commoditized.

Safety note: High-voltage EV components should only be installed by qualified personnel with appropriate EV safety training. Maleon Atlas Parts is a parts sourcing and supply desk, not an installer.
